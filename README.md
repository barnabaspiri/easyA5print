# easyA5print 

Reorder A4 documents to print in a two sided format.

Drop your document and select the pages to reorder. When printed and cut in half along the dashed center mark, placing the Left Pile on top of the Right Pile results in sequentially ordered pages with zero manual collation.

---

## How to Use

1. Double-click `easyA5print.html` to open it in any browser.
2. Select your document.
3. Set your **Start Page** and **End Page**.
4. Click **"Generate and download reordered document"**.

---

## How to Assemble Your Document

1. Print all sheets at 100% scale (simplex / 1-sided).
2. Cut the entire paper stack in half along the dashed center mark.
3. Stack the Left Pile on top of the Right Pile.
4. Finished! All pages are now in 1, 2, 3... sequential order.
